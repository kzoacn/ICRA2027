# Manuscript review notes

The manuscript reports the complete local evaluation: **376/400 (94.0%)**.
See the [evidence map](evidence.md) and [citation audit](citation-audit.md).

The current revision defines the controller state and observation interface,
describes the implemented grasp modes, aligns figure phase labels with recorded
transitions, and distinguishes local stopping from external benchmark success.

The main remaining research additions are component ablations for detector
proposals and carried-object offsets, evaluation on held-out initial states and
assets, calibration sensitivity, and comparisons under matched protocols.
The present evidence supports the measured system with its stated geometric
priors; it does not establish generalization or isolate causal component effects.
